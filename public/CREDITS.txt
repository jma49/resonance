余韵 Resonance — an interactive homage to Ryuichi Sakamoto (1952–2023)
Unofficial tribute. Contains no recordings, likeness, artwork or scores of Ryuichi Sakamoto.
All music is original and generated in the browser.

- Poly Haven HDRIs (CC0): Kiara 1 Dawn, Quarry 01, Venice Sunset, Forest Slope, Dikhololo Night,
  Moonless Golf, Potsdamer Platz, San Giuseppe Bridge — https://polyhaven.com
- NASA Earth Observatory: Black Marble (Earth at Night), Blue Marble — public domain
